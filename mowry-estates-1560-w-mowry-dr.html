FAUSTO COMMERCIAL PRESENTS

Fausto Commercial Realty Consultants is pleased to present Mowry Estates, a residential development opportunity at 1560 W Mowry Drive in Homestead. The offering consists of approximately 2.39 acres of development land with frontage and access along Mowry Drive, surrounded by established residential neighborhoods and positioned within the expanding South Miami-Dade housing market.

The property is offered with previously approved development plans for a boutique community of 10 single-family residences arranged along a private internal roadway, Mowry Court. Available plans include stamped water and sewer engineering drawings depicting proposed utility connections, water mains, sanitary sewer infrastructure, individual service laterals, and related civil improvements. Because substantial planning and engineering work is already complete, the project may offer a purchaser a more direct path toward development — the approved plans are expected to be relatively straightforward to reactivate, subject to confirmation with the applicable agencies, payment of required fees, current code compliance, and any necessary updates.
